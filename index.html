Chapter 10 · The final envelope

A Letter for the Road Ahead

💌
Dear Anshika, I hope you never feel that you have to become less curious, less expressive or less excited about the things you love just to fit into someone else's idea of who you should be. I hope you keep BTS, Jungkook, your music, your interests and all your little sources of happiness close. I hope you keep talking about the things you love without wondering whether somebody will find them silly. I hope you go after your ambitions. I hope you get to become independent, explore places you've wanted to visit, try things that scare you a little, and build a future that feels like it belongs to you. And I hope you remember that changing your plans is allowed too. Also, please don't be too hard on yourself on the days when you don't get everything done. Your worth was never supposed to depend on a perfect day, a result, or how productive you were. You deserve kindness on the ordinary days as much as on the big ones. And because I know I can get very dramatic about you: I really do notice the little things. Your expressions, your sarcasm, your yapping, your random excitement, and the way you can make a simple moment feel memorable. I hope you never lose those parts of yourself. I can't tell you exactly what the future will look like. But I genuinely hope it gives you room to grow, reasons to laugh, people who encourage you, and chances to discover just how much you're capable of. Keep growing, keep glowing, and keep being you. With lots of affection, Someone who wanted your November to feel a little more special 💗
